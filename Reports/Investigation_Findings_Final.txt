10.Simulation Log and Forensic Timeline Validation

The reconstructed forensic timeline was compared with the activity log maintained during the controlled evidence-preparation exercise.

The modification timestamp of Meeting_Notes.txt (21:21:27) was consistent with the approximately recorded modification time of 21:21.

The deleted budget document's filesystem metadata-change timestamp (21:21:36) was consistent with the approximately recorded deletion activity at 21:22. However, this metadata timestamp does not independently establish the exact deletion time.

The Recycle Bin metadata confirmed deletion timestamps of 21:20:06.793 for the original client-list text file and 21:20:43.469 for the original revenue text file.

The replacement CSV documents had creation timestamps of 21:19:57 and 21:20:39, respectively, indicating that the recorded CSV creation events preceded the corresponding Recycle Bin deletion events.

Overall, the examined artifacts were consistent with the controlled simulation. No significant contradictions were identified within the recorded observations. The activity log contained approximate times and did not document the precise timing of every action.

11. METHODS, LIMITATIONS AND CONCLUSIONS
Methods: SHA-256 comparison of VHD copies; Autopsy data-source browsing, file metadata examination, text viewing, deleted-files listing, Recycle Bin artifact investigation and timeline review; manual interpretation of $I bytes (version 2, original size, FILETIME and UTF-16LE original path).
Limitations: This is an intentionally constructed fictional dataset. File timestamps can be affected by filesystem and application behavior; lack of an artifact is not proof of secure erasure. Visible textual recovery is not a full independent validation of every byte. No endpoint USB history, authentication events or user-attribution evidence was collected. The screenshot and report filenames listed above must be checked against files actually saved.
Conclusion: The investigation established recovery of a Shift+Delete budget file (E01), readable allocated business files (E02–E04), and two normally deleted documents retained in the Recycle Bin (E05–E06). The $I files supplied original paths and precise recycle times for E05 and E06. None of the observations establishes real data theft or a responsible individual.
